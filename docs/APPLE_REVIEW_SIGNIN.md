# Dabbler — App Review Sign-In Instructions

This document explains how to sign in to **Dabbler** with the **email + password**
demo account during App Review.

> Dabbler normally uses passwordless sign-in (email one-time code, Google, or
> Apple). For App Review we provide a **dedicated demo account with a password**
> so the reviewer can sign in directly, **without needing to receive an email
> code**.

---

## Demo account credentials

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Email** | `dabbler.pro@proton.me` |
| **Password** | `<ENTER_DEMO_PASSWORD_HERE>` |

> Replace `<ENTER_DEMO_PASSWORD_HERE>` with the demo account password before
> submitting, and enter the same values in **App Store Connect → your app →
> TestFlight → Test Information → Beta App Review Information** (tick
> **"Sign-in required"**).

This account is fully onboarded and contains real content (joined games, posts,
and a complete profile), so all features and functionality are accessible.

---

## How to sign in (step by step)

1. Launch the app. You arrive on the **Welcome** screen.
2. Tap **"Already have an account? Log in"** (below the Continue with
   Email / Google / Apple buttons).
3. On the **Log in** screen, enter:
   - **Email:** `dabbler.pro@proton.me`
   - **Password:** the demo password
4. Tap **Login**.
5. You are signed in and land in the app with full access.

> **Important:** Please sign in with **email + password** (the credentials
> above). Do **not** use "Send email OTP", "Continue with Email",
> "Continue with Google", or "Continue with Apple" — those require receiving a
> one-time code or access to a specific Google/Apple account, which the demo
> account does not need.

---

## Notes for the reviewer

- The demo account already completed onboarding, so it goes straight into the
  app after login.
- No additional verification (SMS/email code) is required for this account when
  using **email + password**.
- If sign-in does not succeed, please contact us via the App Store Connect
  Resolution Center and we will assist promptly.
